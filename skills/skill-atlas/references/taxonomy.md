## Choosing clusters

Aim for 10-16 clusters in 2-3 families. A cluster is something a buyer would pay for as a unit: "Trading systems & execution", not "TypeScript". Languages belong inside `skills`.

Split a cluster when two halves have different buyers (AI agents vs ML model internals). Merge clusters that never appear apart in any project.

Families group clusters for colour only. Good splits: domain / build / people, or research / engineering / go-to-market.

## Depth rubric

| Depth | Meaning | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Shipped and operated in production, repeatedly | Live service with users or money, on-call history |
| 4 | Shipped with tests, users or published results | Public beta, package on a registry, research with a written verdict |
| 3 | Built working projects end to end | Several complete repos, hackathon entries |
| 2 | Prototypes, coursework, training | Tutorials, phase-0 builds, a degree in progress |
| 1 | Exposure only | Read about it, one experiment |

Score the strongest evidence, then drop a point if it is all more than two years old.

## Fields and weights

A field is a market where people hire: crypto trading, health tech, developer tools. 12-18 fields is typical for a broad builder; 6-10 for a specialist.

Weight per cluster: 3 the field cannot be done without it, 2 used weekly, 1 occasionally useful, 0 irrelevant. A field with total fit under about a third of the maximum is a stretch field; keep it only if the person cares about it.

## Roles and tiers

Write roles as real job titles that appear on job boards. For each, list the 2-5 clusters it draws on and the fields it lives in.

| Tier | Test |
|---|---|
| 3 Strong | Projects match most lines of a typical post for this title, at a credible scale |
| 2 Good | Real proof exists, with a gap in scale, production use or seniority (write it in `gap`) |
| 1 Stretch | One more project, credential or public record away (write which in `gap`) |

When unsure between two tiers, choose the lower one. People forgive a modest atlas; they do not forgive one that collapses in an interview.

## Combos, services and research

- **Combos:** 2-5 clusters where every one has proof and the overlap is rare. Name the buyer and one concrete offer.
- **Services:** group into layers from highest value to own products. Bands are estimates; say so in `priceNote`. Name the project that proves each service.
- **Research:** question, method, result, next step. Put negative and neutral results in plainly. Use `open` for a question the person wants to discuss.
- **Patterns:** 4-7 observations about how the person works, each tied to projects. Include one risk.
